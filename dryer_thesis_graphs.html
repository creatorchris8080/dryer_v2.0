Time (min) · Heat transfer rate to air (W)
Predicted convective and radiative transfer from the quartz heaters to chamber air.
Model scope: These are reduced-order predictions from the simplified 3D Boussinesq model parameters, not validated CFD or experimental measurements. Heater and chamber temperatures use a coupled energy balance; the spatial profiles, outlet split, pressure proxy, and heat-flux estimate use stated simplifying assumptions. Use instrumented measurements or a validated solver for calibrated results.
